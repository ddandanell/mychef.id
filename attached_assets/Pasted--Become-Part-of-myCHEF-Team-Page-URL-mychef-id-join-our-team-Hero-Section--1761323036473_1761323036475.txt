## **FAQ Section**

**Do I need certificates or diplomas?**
No! We're interested in your skills and experience, not paperwork.

**I'm a foreigner - can I apply?**
Yes! We welcome all nationalities. Just make sure you have the right to work in Indonesia.

**I don't have much experience, can I still apply?**
If you're talented and passionate, yes! We consider people with 1+ year experience or exceptional home cooks.

**Do you only need people in Bali?**
No! We operate across Indonesia - Bali, Jakarta, Surabaya, Lombok, and expanding to more cities.

**What if I can cook multiple cuisines?**
Perfect! We love versatile chefs. Select all cuisines you're confident cooking.

**I'm a server, not a chef - do you need me?**
Yes! We need great service staff for events and larger bookings.

**When will I hear back?**
We review applications within 2-3 days and contact promising candidates via WhatsApp.

**Is this full-time work?**
Flexible! Some people work with us part-time, others build full-time careers. You choose.
