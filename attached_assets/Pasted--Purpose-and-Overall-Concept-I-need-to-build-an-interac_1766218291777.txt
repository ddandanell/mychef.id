# 🎯 Purpose and Overall Concept

I need to build an interactive, user-friendly price calculator for my website [mychef.id](http://mychef.id), which makes it quick, easy, and transparent for customers to:

- Calculate accurate prices for their chef visits
- Understand exactly what they're paying for
- Customize their experience according to their wishes
- Book directly without delays or uncertainty

**Key Principles:**

- **Full Transparency**: The customer should always know what the price includes
- **Flexibility**: Ability to customize for each individual guest
- **Simplicity**: Easy to navigate, even though there are many options
- **Precision**: Correct prices based on number of people, courses, ingredient quality, and chef's time

---

### **Price System - Explanation (Info box):**

<aside>
💡

**How Your Price Is Calculated:**

**Basic Formula:**

- **Ingredients**: [Selected level] × [Number of adults] + ([Selected level] × 0.5 × [Number of children 5-10 years])
- **Chef's Time**: [Hourly rate] × [Number of hours based on courses]
- **Shopping time**: Standard 2 hours for consultation + shopping (included)

**Example:**

- 5 adults + 2 children (5-10 years)
- 4 courses
- Premium ingredients

**Calculation:**

- Ingredients: (375,000 × 5) + (375,000 × 0.5 × 2) = 1,875,000 + 375,000 = Rp 2,250,000
- Chef time: 4 hours × Rp 200,000/hour = Rp 800,000
- Consultation/shopping: Included
- **Total**: Rp 3,050,000

*Children 0-4 years: FREE (no cost)*

- **0-4 years**: Free (no calculation)

- Standard hourly rate: Rp 200,000/hour

- 2 hours consultation + shopping (no extra cost)
