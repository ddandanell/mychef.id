**Expandable accordion questions:**

**How much does a private chef really cost?**
It's more affordable than most people think! Chef service starts at Rp 800,000 per hour, and most dinners are 3-4 hours. So for a dinner party of 6, you're looking at around Rp 2,500,000-3,500,000 for the chef, plus ingredients (maybe Rp 1,500,000-2,500,000 depending on what you choose). That's Rp 4,000,000-6,000,000 total, or about Rp 666,000-1,000,000 per person - similar to a nice restaurant, but WAY more special! And the more guests you have, the more affordable it becomes per person.

**Why are ingredients charged separately?**
Great question! We separate chef service from ingredients for transparency and flexibility. This way, YOU control your budget. Want to keep it affordable with local ingredients? Perfect! Want to splurge on wagyu and lobster? Go for it! You see exactly what you're paying for. We'll provide shopping lists and cost estimates, or we can help source everything for you. You're in control!

**How do I book a chef?**
Super easy! Just click any WhatsApp button and message us at +62 822-3756-5997. Tell us:
- Your date and location (which villa/area)
- Number of guests
- Cuisine preference (or ask for suggestions!)
- Your approximate budget

We'll match you with available chefs who'll send menu proposals with pricing. Chat back and forth until it's perfect, then confirm the booking!

**Can I see prices before I contact you?**
Absolutely! That's why we show starting rates on each chef's profile. But for your exact quote (including service hours and ingredient estimates), WhatsApp us your details. We'll send complete pricing within 30 minutes. No obligation - we want you to know exactly what you're getting!

**What if I want to buy ingredients myself?**
That's totally fine! Your chef will provide a detailed shopping list with specific quantities and recommended brands. Many guests enjoy shopping at local markets - it's part of the Bali experience! Just make sure everything is ready when the chef arrives. Or we can coordinate delivery if you prefer.

**Can you help me get the ingredients?**
Yes! We work with trusted suppliers across Bali. We can coordinate delivery to your villa, suggest the best markets, or handle the entire shopping process. You'll get receipts so you see exactly what everything costs. Whatever is easiest for you!

**What areas do you serve in Bali?**
Everywhere in Bali! Seminyak, Canggu, Ubud, Sanur, Nusa Dua, Uluwatu, Jimbaran - we've got chefs ready. We also serve Jakarta, Surabaya, Lombok, and other Indonesian cities. Very remote locations might have a small travel fee, but we'll tell you upfront.

**Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?**
Of course! Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, allergies - just tell us. Our chefs will create perfect menus around your needs. It doesn't change the hourly rate, though ingredient costs might vary slightly.

**How far in advance should I book?**
For best chef availability, we recommend 3-5 days ahead. But we often handle last-minute requests (even 24-48 hours) if chefs are available! Peak season (July-August, December) books faster, so reach out early. Message us on WhatsApp to check availability for your date.

**What if my villa has a basic kitchen?**
No worries! Our chefs work in all kinds of villa kitchens. They bring their own professional knives and can adapt to basic facilities. We've created amazing meals in the simplest kitchens. Just let us know what's available when you book.

**Do I need to provide anything?**
Just a working kitchen with basic equipment (stove/oven, pots/pans, plates/utensils). The chef brings professional knives and specialty tools. If your villa is missing something essential, we'll let you know ahead of time. Most villas have everything needed!

**What about drinks and wine?**
Beverages are separate from both chef service and food ingredients. We can recommend perfect wine pairings and help source them, but you'll purchase those separately. Some chefs offer wine service/sommelier expertise (ask when booking!).

**Can we learn to cook with the chef?**
Yes! Many chefs offer interactive cooking experiences where you cook alongside them. This is usually the same hourly rate, just a different style of service. Perfect for groups who want a fun, hands-on experience. Ask about it on WhatsApp!

**When do I pay?**
Typically 50% deposit secures your booking, with 50% paid 24-48 hours before service. For ingredients (if we're sourcing), those are paid before shopping begins. All details in your booking confirmation. We accept bank transfer, credit cards, and cash (Rupiah).

**What if I need to cancel?**
We're flexible! 
- Cancel 72+ hours ahead: Full refund of chef service fee
- Cancel 48-72 hours: 50% refund
- Cancel under 48 hours: No refund (unless emergency)

*Note: Ingredients already purchased can't be refunded, but can often be delivered to you.*

**Do the chefs speak English?**
Yes! All myCHEF chefs speak English fluently. Many also speak Bahasa Indonesia, and some know French, Italian, or other languages.

**Can you handle big groups?**
Absolutely! We've done villa parties for 50+ people. For groups over 12, we typically add service staff (around Rp 300,000-400,000 per helper). For really large events, we can coordinate multiple chefs. WhatsApp us for big party quotes!

**Do I tip the chef?**
Gratuity isn't required but is always appreciated! If you loved the experience, 10-15% of the chef service fee is customary. You can give tips directly to the chef in cash at the end of service.

**Is this actually worth it vs. going to a restaurant?**
